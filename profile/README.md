# Dytomic

**Independent game development studio in Montreal, Quebec. Home of [Pirate Raids Online (PRO)](./pro-web), an old-school inspired MMORPG for PC set in the world of Ascaron, where Demigods lead the Pirate Coalition against the tyrannical Navy. Four playable races: Dwarf, Triton, Runeborn, and Amazonian.**

> **Status:** development paused since early 2024. All repositories here are archived and preserved as a public record of the work that was done.

## About Pirate Raids Online

Pirate Raids Online is a PC MMORPG set in the world of Ascaron. Players take on the role of a Demigod leading the Pirate Coalition in a rebellion against the tyrannical forces of The Navy. The game's design emphasizes old-school MMORPG values (grinding, guilds, open world PvP, raid encounters) modernized with contemporary UX and performance expectations.

**Playable races:** Dwarf · Triton · Runeborn · Amazonian

The full product vision, story, and visual language lives in the [pro-web repository](./pro-web). The earlier social-media splash page is preserved in [pro-splash](./pro-splash).
